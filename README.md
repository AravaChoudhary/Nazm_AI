# Nazm.AI 

It is an AI-powered poetry companion that helps poets and writers craft beautiful verses with ease. Whether you're searching for the perfect rhyme, composing your next couplet, or simply exploring poetic emotions, Nazm.AI is here to inspire your creative journey.

## ✨ Features

- 🖋️ **Poetic Line Completion**: Generate the next line of your poem in your chosen tone and style.
- 🎙️ **Emotion Detection**: Analyze your poetry to discover its underlying emotional essence.
- 🔁 **Rhyme Suggestions**: Get rhyming words and phrases that match the poetic flow.
- 📝 **Meter Checking** *(optional)*: Identify rhythm and syllable patterns to maintain poetic structure.
- 🧠 **Multilingual Poetry Support**: Write in English, Hindi, or Urdu (more languages coming soon).
- 💬 **Voice-to-Poetry** *(optional)*: Convert your spoken words into poetic form.
